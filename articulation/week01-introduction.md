## The task

4. **Commit** this file in your pull request.

Due before Session 02. Your mentor announces the date.

## The structure: five blocks, answer first

1. **Name and anchor.** "I'm ___, and right now I'm ___." (for example "in my final year of ___ at ___", or "working as ___")
2. **Answer first.** "I joined this Fellowship to ___."
3. **Past.** "For example, I built / fixed / learned ___, and it taught me ___."
4. **Present.** "Right now I'm strongest at ___, and I want to get better at ___."
5. **Next step.** "This week I'll ___. One thing I'd like help with: ___."

Aim for about five sentences.
